# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, stay focused on the work,
and make technical disagreements concrete and evidence-based.

Examples of constructive behavior:

- Give feedback on code, documentation, and decisions rather than people.
- Provide reproduction steps, references, or rationale when raising issues.
- Respect project scope, licensing, and source attribution.
- Avoid harassment, personal attacks, or disclosure of private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and contributions that violate
these standards. Repeated or severe violations may result in restricted
participation in the project.
